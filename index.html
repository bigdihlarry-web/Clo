Game Rules

  • Pre-roll: Each player rolls one die. Highest roll goes first.
  • Main Roll: Players take turns rolling three dice.
  • 4-5-6: Instant win!
  • 1-2-3: Instant lose!
  • Triple 1: Both players re-roll the round.
  • Triples: Three of a kind (ranked by number).
  • Pairs: Ranked by the singleton die value.
  • Singles: Ranked by highest die value.
  • Ties: Both players re-roll the round.
